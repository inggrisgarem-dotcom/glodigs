About Me

Built on experience.
Delivered with transparency.

Bari VijayaFounder & Lead Designer

Glodigs is a one-person studio with a deliberately narrow promise: I build websites that earn their keep. Not showpieces — working assets that bring in enquiries.

I started as an assistant at a partner studio, supporting website delivery for clients across Indonesia and the Netherlands. That's where I learned what actually matters once a site is live: it has to load fast, read clearly on a phone, and make the next step obvious. I now work independently and take projects end to end.

I write my own HTML, CSS and JavaScript rather than assembling pages from plugins and page builders. It takes longer, and it's the reason the sites I build stay fast and don't break when something external goes down.

No templates. No shortcuts. Every project is built for performance, structure, and long-term growth.

The Journey

Milestones so far.

Not a highlight reel — a record of what was actually built, tested, and sometimes rejected along the way.

Started as an assistant

Supported website delivery for a partner studio across Indonesian and Dutch markets, before going independent.

Inherited a broken export, rebuilt from zero

A site export that failed without external assets was diagnosed and rebuilt as a single self-contained document — the studio's own first proof of the standard client work is held to.

Six demos shipped, source code open

Independent case studies built end-to-end, each with a live demo and published source — not just screenshots.

A flawed showreel was rejected, not shipped

An AI-generated video with garbled on-screen text was flagged and pulled before publishing, then rebuilt from real material.

47-point audit, zero fails

Navigation, every modal, video playback, and five screen widths tested with a headless browser — not eyeballed.

Now: outreach over polish

The site is intentionally frozen for routine changes. Studio focus has moved to reaching the first paid clients directly.
