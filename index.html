Why Work With Me

I carry the risk, not you.

I'm building this studio independently, and I'd rather earn trust with terms than with claims. Here's what that means in practice.

◈

You pay after approval

No deposit, no upfront fee. You see the finished website first. If it doesn't meet what we agreed, you don't pay — the risk sits with me, which is where a new working relationship should start.

◉

You talk to the person building it

No account manager, no handoffs, no message passed through three people. Every question goes straight to the person writing the code, and every answer comes back the same way.

▤

Every decision is documented

You receive a written build record: what was made, why it was made that way, and what the measured results are. You own the source code and the documentation at the end — not a locked account.

◐

Scope agreed before work starts

Pages, features, timeline and revision rounds are written down and confirmed before a single line is written. No moving targets, no invoice surprises at the end.

About Me

Built on experience.
Delivered with transparency.

Bari VijayaFounder & Lead Designer

I run Glodigs as a one-person studio with a deliberately narrow promise: I build websites that earn their keep. Not showpieces — working assets that bring in enquiries.

I started as an assistant at a partner studio, supporting website delivery for clients across Indonesia and the Netherlands. That's where I learned what actually matters once a site is live: it has to load fast, read clearly on a phone, and make the next step obvious. I now work independently and take projects end to end.

I write my own HTML, CSS and JavaScript rather than assembling pages from plugins and page builders. It takes longer, and it's the reason the sites I build stay fast and don't break when something external goes down.

No templates. No shortcuts. Every project is built for performance, structure, and long-term growth.
