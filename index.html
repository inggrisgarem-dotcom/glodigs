Why Work With Me

I carry the risk,
not you.

I'm building this studio independently, and I'd rather earn trust with terms than with claims. Here's what that means in practice.

No deposit, no upfront fee. You see the finished website first. If it doesn't meet what we agreed, you don't pay — the risk sits with me, which is where a new working relationship should start.

No account manager, no handoffs, no message passed through three people. Every question goes straight to the person writing the code, and every answer comes back the same way.

You receive a written build record: what was made, why it was made that way, and what the measured results are. You own the source code and the documentation at the end — not a locked account.

Pages, features, timeline and revision rounds are written down and confirmed before a single line is written. No moving targets, no invoice surprises at the end.
